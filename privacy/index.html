Dragon Time Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This policy explains how Dragon Time handles information when you use the game. Dragon Time is offline-first and currently has no developer-operated account system, analytics service, cloud database, or backend. Some optional or connected features use Google services that may process data under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Information stored on your device

Dragon Time uses local app storage (AsyncStorage) for:

  • game records and aggregate statistics, including score, survival time, maximum speed, maximum length, games played, and collected items;
  • sound, music, vibration, language, and other local preferences;
  • the selected language or the choice to follow the device language;
  • first-launch and last-known timestamps used for the local 24-hour offer window;
  • a local cached entitlement indicating that remove_ads was purchased;
  • the highest leaderboard score waiting to synchronize when Play Games is unavailable.

This information is not sent to a Dragon Time backend because no such backend currently exists. It generally remains until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Reinstalling or clearing data may remove local records and preferences. A qualifying purchase may be restorable through Google Play.

Advertising, AdMob, and consent

Dragon Time uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for banner, interstitial, and voluntary rewarded advertisements. Before requesting ads where applicable, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to update consent information and display Google's official consent form when required. If UMP indicates that privacy options must remain available, the app shows a Privacy entry that reopens the official form.

The current app configuration requests non-personalized ads. Depending on consent, region, device settings, SDK configuration, and Google's limited-ads rules, Google may limit processing or decide that ads cannot be requested. Refusing consent where that option is available does not block gameplay. Ads may be unavailable while offline.

According to Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosure, the SDK may automatically collect or share information such as IP address (which may indicate general location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, advertising ID, app set ID, and other applicable device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement/analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Data sent by the SDK is handled by Google and is not stored in a Dragon Time backend.

Purchasing Remove Ads hides banner and interstitial ads. Voluntary rewarded ads used for Continue remain available, so Google Mobile Ads may still be used after that purchase.

Purchases and Google Play Billing

Dragon Time uses Google Play Billing for the one-time product remove_ads. Google Play processes payment details and the purchase through the user's Google account. Dragon Time receives the product identifier, purchase status, and purchase token or transaction information needed to grant, finish, and restore the entitlement. The app stores only a local cached entitlement and does not send purchase information to a developer-operated backend.

Prices, currency, eligibility, and any promotional offer are supplied by Google Play. The app's local first-launch clock only determines whether it may display an eligible 24-hour offer; it does not create a price or establish purchase eligibility.

Google Play Games and rankings

When Google Play Games is configured and available, Dragon Time may use it to sign in, submit the player's best score, retrieve the player's display name or alias, score, and rank, and open Google's leaderboard interface. Google handles Play Games account information and the global leaderboard. If synchronization is unavailable, Dragon Time stores the highest pending score locally and tries again later. The developer does not operate a separate ranking server.

Diagnostics and offline use

Google SDKs may process diagnostic and performance information as described in their documentation. Dragon Time also writes SDK status and error messages to device logs in development or diagnostic builds. These app logs are not uploaded to a developer-operated backend.

Core gameplay, local records, preferences, and language selection work offline. Ads, consent updates, purchases, purchase restoration, and Play Games features depend on the relevant Google service and network availability. Their failure does not block core gameplay.

Data sharing and third-party services

The developer does not directly sell local gameplay records or upload them to a proprietary service. Information may be processed by Google when the corresponding SDK or service is used. Relevant resources:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Technologies
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure
  • Google Play Terms of Service
  • Google Play Games Services

Google's retention, security, and account controls apply to information processed by Google services.

Your choices

Depending on availability and region, you may review choices through the official UMP Privacy form, control the Android advertising ID through device settings, manage Google account and Play Games settings, avoid voluntary rewarded ads, clear local app data, uninstall the app, or purchase remove_ads to remove banner and interstitial advertising.

Children

Dragon Time does not currently ask for a date of birth and does not automatically mark users as under the age of consent. The final audience and age classification must be configured accurately in Google Play and AdMob before publication. This policy must be reviewed if the app is later directed to children or if age-assurance features are added.
